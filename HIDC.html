Healthcare Infrastructure Development Centre

Designing, advising & implementing sustainable healthcare infrastructure for hospitals, labs and public health facilities worldwide.

About Us

The Healthcare Infrastructure Development Centre (HIDC) integrates planning, engineering, operations and training to create healthcare environments that are resilient, patient-centred and cost-effective. We partner with governments, hospitals and private organisations worldwide.

Our Services

End-to-end consultancy for healthcare infrastructure — planning, construction, operations and compliance.

Master Planning & Design

Site evaluation, master planning, zoning and phased development roadmaps that minimise disruption.

Construction & Vendor Rating

Tendering support, vendor evaluation, quality assurance and construction oversight.

Operations & Maintenance

Operational manuals, maintenance planning, training and performance monitoring.

Biomedical & Waste Management

Safe handling, segregation and disposal systems for medical, biomedical and radioactive waste.

Training & Capacity Building

Custom programs for paramedics, maintenance staff and hospital administrators.

Compliance & Accreditation Support

Assist in grading, accreditation and regulatory compliance for national & international standards.

Our Aim

  • • Advise on nurturing & developing healthcare infrastructure
  • • Develop paramedics and maintenance staff
  • • Introduce modern systems for upkeep and operations
  • • Rate construction agencies and suppliers
  • • Manage medical and biomedical waste

Objectives

  • • Central organisation for infrastructure providers
  • • Learner-centred practical knowledge
  • • Evaluation & grading of providers
  • • Business models for efficient facility development
